# Design direction

Use the approved eleven-section sequence: commercial shop hero, operating context, product explanation, suitability, measurements, shop example, plans, four-step assessment, team and reference enquiries, FAQ, contact and footer.

Navy #0b2638; warm paper #f8f7f2; accessible teal #008279; pale teal #e7f3f1; amber #f5bd42. Local Manrope variable font, generous section spacing, flat comparison layouts, precise line icons and strong photography. Mobile stacks narrative sections and keeps the premises picker compact.

Motion supports reading: 12px reveals, once-only image wipes, simultaneous bill bars with their actual ratio, a four-step progress line, limited desktop parallax and 220ms accordions. No scroll hijacking. Reduced-motion preference removes motion. Static content remains readable without JavaScript.

Buttons either navigate, reveal information or prepare an actual contact message. The form validates information and produces a visitor-reviewed WhatsApp message. Nothing is sent automatically.

The gpt-taste motion update uses locally served GSAP 3.15.0 and ScrollTrigger to apply SCROLLING-EFFECTS.md. The approved section order, photography, Manrope font and natural scrolling take priority over alternative layout and pinning suggestions. The hero uses a wider desktop measure and a smaller phone size, verified at no more than three lines in both languages. The premises grid uses dense placement and contains no empty grid cells.
